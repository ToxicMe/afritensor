About Afritensor Consult

Complementary professional disciplines, working as one firm to give organisations practical, informed and tailored solutions.

Who we are

Afritensor Consult is a professional services and technology consultancy. Our work spans finance and audit, software development and data analysis, debt management and collection, taxation and payroll, and corporate training.

Because these disciplines sit within one firm, clients benefit from advice that connects accounting, compliance, systems and people.

Our approach

We start by understanding the organisation, its obligations and its constraints. We then recommend solutions that are proportionate, clearly explained and ready to implement.

Where a matter crosses disciplines, the relevant specialists work together under one engagement.
